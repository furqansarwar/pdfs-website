JPG to PDF Online

Combine image files into one PDF document with PDFS JPG to PDF. It is useful for turning photographed pages, receipts, forms, or image scans into a single file that is easier to send and store.

Use JPG to PDF
Before you start: The Image to PDF workspace supports JPG, JPEG, and PNG images and lets you arrange the selected pages before conversion. Review the page order and image orientation before downloading the finished PDF.

How to jpg to pdf

  1. Open the Image to PDF tool on the PDFS homepage.
  2. Choose multiple JPG, JPEG, or PNG images from your device.
  3. Arrange the selected images in the order you want using the available controls.
  4. Select Convert to PDF and download the combined document.

What this PDF tool does

The Image to PDF workspace supports JPG, JPEG, and PNG images and lets you arrange the selected pages before conversion. Review the page order and image orientation before downloading the finished PDF.

Important limitations

The output is a PDF containing your images. Text inside a photo remains image content and is not automatically converted into selectable text. Very large images may create a larger PDF file.

Frequently asked questions

Which image formats can I use?

The Image to PDF tool lists JPG, JPEG, and PNG as supported formats.

Can I combine multiple pictures?

Yes. Select multiple images and arrange them in the order you want.

Will text in photos become editable?

No. The images are placed into a PDF; OCR text recognition is not included.
