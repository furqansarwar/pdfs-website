Protect PDF Online

Add password-based encryption to a PDF with PDFS Protect PDF. This tool is useful when you want to add an extra layer of protection before sharing a document. You set and confirm the password before processing.

Use Protect PDF
Before you start: PDFS uses an AES-256 encryption engine in the browser for this tool. Choose a strong, unique password and share it with the intended recipient through a separate secure channel. Keep a safe copy of the password because PDFS cannot recover it for you.

How to protect pdf

  1. Open Protect PDF from the PDFS homepage.
  2. Select the PDF you want to encrypt.
  3. Enter a password of at least six characters and confirm it.
  4. Choose Protect PDF, then download the encrypted document.

What this PDF tool does

PDFS uses an AES-256 encryption engine in the browser for this tool. Choose a strong, unique password and share it with the intended recipient through a separate secure channel. Keep a safe copy of the password because PDFS cannot recover it for you.

Important limitations

The tool encrypts the selected PDF and does not provide password recovery. Do not forget the password. Encryption is not a guarantee against every possible security risk; use trusted practices for highly sensitive documents.

Frequently asked questions

What password length is required?

The tool requires at least six characters and asks you to confirm the password.

What encryption does PDFS use?

The current Protect PDF tool uses AES-256 encryption in the browser.

Can PDFS recover a forgotten password?

No. Keep your password safe; it cannot be recovered by PDFS.
