Merge PDF Online

Combine several PDF documents into one file with the free PDFS Merge PDF tool. It is useful when reports, forms, invoices, or scanned documents arrive as separate PDFs and you need one document in a chosen order.

Use Merge PDF
Before you start: Merge PDF is designed for combining documents without needing to upload them to a PDFS account. Choose the files in the order you want them to appear. Before sharing the result, review the final document to confirm that all pages are present and in the expected sequence.

How to merge pdf

  1. Open the Merge PDF tool from the PDFS homepage.
  2. Select two or more PDF files from your device. You can choose multiple files together.
  3. Check the selected file order, then continue to create one combined PDF.
  4. Download the merged PDF and open it to check the page sequence.

What this PDF tool does

Merge PDF is designed for combining documents without needing to upload them to a PDFS account. Choose the files in the order you want them to appear. Before sharing the result, review the final document to confirm that all pages are present and in the expected sequence.

Important limitations

The merge tool combines the selected PDF files. It does not rewrite the text inside them or guarantee that interactive forms, unusual annotations, or every document feature will behave identically in all PDF readers.

Frequently asked questions

Can I merge more than two PDFs?

Yes. Select multiple PDF files in the file picker and combine them into one document.

Can I change the order of PDFs?

Choose the files in the sequence you want before processing. Review the output after downloading.

Are my PDFs uploaded to PDFS?

The PDFS tools are designed for browser-based processing. Your files are processed on your device where supported; third-party libraries may load from their CDNs.
