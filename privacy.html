Privacy Policy

Understand what happens to your information when you use PDFS.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how PDFS (“we”, “our” or “the website”) handles information when you visit https://furqansarwar.github.io/pdfs-website/ and use its available tools.

2. Files you select

PDFS tools are designed to process supported documents inside your browser. The website's tool code does not intentionally upload the files you select to a PDFS account or a PDFS-operated file storage server. Your files remain on your device while the browser performs the supported operation.

Processing may depend on third-party JavaScript libraries loaded from content delivery networks (CDNs). Those providers may receive ordinary technical connection information when your browser requests their libraries. Do not use this website for highly confidential documents unless you are comfortable with these arrangements.

3. Technical information and hosting

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, date and time of requests, and security or diagnostic logs under their own policies. PDFS analytics records page views, tool interactions, a temporary browser-session identifier, browser and device category, and approximate country, region and city when available. PDFS does not store visitors’ raw IP addresses in its analytics database.

4. Cookies and analytics

PDFS uses session storage in your browser to group page views and tool interactions during a browser session. It is not used to identify you by name. Approximate location is estimated from your public IP address through the IPWho.is geolocation service. That provider receives the IP address to perform the lookup and applies its own privacy practices. The resulting country, region, city and time-zone fields may be stored with the session analytics. PDFS does not save the raw IP address. City-level results are estimates and may be inaccurate or unavailable, especially when VPNs, proxies or mobile networks are used.

5. Third-party services

PDFS uses GitHub Pages for hosting, Supabase for database services, IPWho.is for approximate IP geolocation, and third-party providers for fonts and software libraries. When your browser connects to these providers, they may receive technical connection information under their own privacy policies. Please review the relevant provider terms and privacy notices before using the website.

6. Children's privacy

PDFS is a general-purpose document utility website and is not specifically directed at children. Please do not submit personal information about a child through any future contact or account feature.

7. Security and your responsibility

We take reasonable care when maintaining the website, but no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep backup copies of important documents and use trusted offline software for documents requiring a high level of confidentiality.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website's features or services change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates the latest revision. Continued use of the website after an update means you have had an opportunity to review the revised policy.

9. Contact

You can send a message through the Contact & Support form on the PDFS website. Your name, email address, subject and message are stored in the PDFS support inbox so the administrator can review and respond. Do not include passwords, payment details or highly sensitive personal information in the form.
